about this projects

"Swim untile the sea blue" is a tabletop strategy and survival game that combines competition and environmental education. Players take on the roles of various marine creatures, navigating the challenges of food collection and pollution avoidance to climb the food chain. The ultimate goal is to evolve into the apex predator of the ocean and secure victory.

Gameplay Features

Gameplay Features

During the game, players must balance survival with strategic interactions, competing against others while adapting to environmental hazards. The core mechanism revolves around evolution, with players progressing through ecological tiers by making thoughtful decisions about resource collection and risk management.

Inspired by real marine ecosystems, the game’s design incorporates realistic marine creatures and habitats, offering players an immersive "virtual ocean journey." While enjoying the gameplay, players can develop a deeper curiosity and appreciation for ocean culture, sparking an interest in marine conservation or even inspiring real-world exploration of the ocean.
