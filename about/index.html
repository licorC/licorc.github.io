About

Who am i?

I’m Chang Ziyue, an artist passionate about exploration and creation.

I graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Digital Media Art from Shenzhen University. Online, I’ve also gone by the names licor_chang, Licor, or “Chang Zui.”

I specialize in 2D art, character design, and storytelling. In the future, I hope to dedicate myself to the things I love, using art and narrative as mediums to express emotions and touch the hearts of others.

About My

University

During my time at university, I experienced a unique and enriching academic journey. Initially, I found myself in the Product Design program by chance while also pursuing a minor in Digital Media Art. In 2022, during my junior year, I officially transferred to the Digital Media Art program. Despite the challenges of balancing a demanding schedule that required me to take courses across different academic years due to the school’s curriculum structure for transfer and minor students, I consistently maintained a high level of academic performance. My GPA stands at 3.81, and I ranked among the top 10 in my program throughout.

This diverse academic background has allowed me to gain extensive knowledge in both art and design while developing adaptable learning skills and a multidisciplinary perspective. These experiences have significantly enriched my creative potential and broadened the horizons of my work.

My Journey

in Art

Art has always been at the core of my life. I picked up a paintbrush at the age of 6, guided by a friend, and by the age of 12, I had firmly decided to pursue art as my lifelong career. Since 2017, I’ve been accepting art commissions online, completing over 50 projects worth thousands of yuan each, and growing a social media following of 20,000.

This experience has transformed me from an enthusiastic hobbyist into a professional artist. It has also deepened my appreciation for the joy of collaboration and the fulfillment of expressing myself through my work.

About Games and

My Creative Philosophy

My journey with games began in 2014. Prior to that, I was captivated by classic AVG (adventure) games. Over time, I explored a wider variety of game genres, including Overwatch, Dark Souls, Metro, Assassin’s Creed, Detroit: Become Human, Disco Elysium, Sunless Sea, and Baldur’s Gate 3. Despite the diversity of games, I’ve always been particularly drawn to their narrative and artistic aspects. It was games like Disco Elysium, with its rich, immersive storytelling, that first inspired me to consider creating games myself. 

As a female gamer, I’ve often felt the weight of gender stereotypes in gaming culture. Many people assume that women only enjoy dress-up or dating simulation games. However, I believe that games, as an art form, should not be defined by gender. Regardless of gender, everyone can appreciate deep narratives and emotional expression, rather than simply seeking flashy CGI sequences or fast-paced combat. Gameplay mechanics should not become a tool to differentiate players based on gender.

With this belief in mind, I’ve begun exploring the world of game development. My goal is to create more narrative-driven, strategy-based, and puzzle-oriented games that offer players a slower, more thoughtful, and immersive experience. These games would not only entertain but also serve as platforms for emotional expression and intellectual engagement. Through my work, I hope to return games to their universal nature, much like literature, painting, or film, and make them a form of artistic creation for all humans, not defined by gender.
