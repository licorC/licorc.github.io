Last 1:00 minutes

This is an animated short film pilot based on the current situation in today's society.
In today's society dependence on mobile phones and other electronic devices has become commonplace, and addiction in the virtual world will lead to neglect of the real world. Therefore, I used a dual parallel narrative in the animation, the title ‘The Last Minute’ is both the so-called last minute when swiping a mobile phone and the last minute of another person's life at the same time. The value of time, which we often overlook, may become a little more apparent when the same events are compared.

canvas-animated adverts

This animation is an entry for the National Student Advertising Competition, targeting the brand ‘Canvas’.
When designing the brand slogan, I started from the core competitiveness of ‘Convenience’ and ‘Convenience’, and showed a reverse scenario with the intention of letting the audience intuitively feel the pain point of the current situation without the help of this product.
This work finally won the second prize at the provincial level. As a first attempt to use animation works, now review seems, if increase the use of the product before and after the comparison of the show may be able to get better results.
