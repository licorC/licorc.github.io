about this projects


'DARK FOREST' is a tabletop psycho-competitive game. In the game, players will play the role of the host who dominates the game process or the hunters who hunt each other in the forest.

During the game, miscommunication, chains of suspicion, and random crises will affect the hunters' actions, making them think carefully about every move and the timing and risk of attacking in order to prevent themselves from being exposed in the eyes of another hunter.
