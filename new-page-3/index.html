about this projects

In this game, player needs to takes on the role of a scientist who creates a nuclear bomb and decides to launch it. The player must create the bomb using the least amount of resources and launch it according to the Führer's orders.

The player will use basic shapes such as triangles, squares, overlays, rotations, scaling, and other effects to create shapes that correspond to the bombing zones given by the Führer.

Once ready, the player presses the launch button, and the fate of all mankind lies in their hands.

game play

design

Players can input the basic shape and other components to modify the shape.

The right-hand slot performs an in-computation on the left-hand computation result. The rightmost slot outputs the final result.

player can use each component to create the same shape as the required blast zone.

game art

design

When designing the visual style for this game, I drew significant inspiration from Papers, Please and 60 Seconds!, as their styles and narratives closely align with the tone of this project. Both games feature dark storylines—dystopian themes and nuclear war—yet manage to incorporate elements of dark humor. Additionally, I infused the design with the minimalist, hand-drawn style of Rick and Morty.

During the design process, I truly realized how much Papers, Please has influenced me. I’ve always been fond of the workbench + first-person interaction style UI, and judging by current trends, it’s becoming increasingly iconic.
