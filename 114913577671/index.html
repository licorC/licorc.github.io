Level design

Level design

The game uses height as a standard to separate the levels, and at different height intervals, the map will generate different map blocks and trigger special events or challenges. As the altitude increases, the difficulty of the game will gradually increase. Players should choose the right climbing strategy before setting out to get to the highest point possible.

Event-related

mechanism

As the core of the game, event triggering will be triggered by altitude (level), snow state, and time as three factors.

Character

design

The characters in the game will be divided into different functions. In addition to the "Leader" that the player can operate, the npc will be divided into three functions: "medical staff", "guide" and "scholar", and provide different help.
Here's an example of a design for one of the medical staff.

related

mechanics

related

mechanics

The character has two numerical attributes, health and morale (sanity), which decrease due to the failure of battles, event challenges, and player decisions. The drop in sanity will cause the character's image to change. Such changes are irreversible.
